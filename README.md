# Short-term prediction of severe anemia in critically ill patients

Code for data extraction, preprocessing, modelling, and figure generation accompanying the manuscript:

> **Short-term prediction of severe anemia in critically ill patients using machine learning: Development and external validation**
> Lorenz Kapral\*, Aylin Albrecht\*, Fabian P. Hammerle, Stefan Bartos, Paul Lichtenegger, Mathias Maleczek, Sebastian Zeiner, Stefan J. Schaller, Oliver Kimberger, David M. Baron
> Medical University of Vienna, Department of Anaesthesia, Intensive Care Medicine and Pain Medicine
> *(\*contributed equally)*

The study develops a machine-learning model that predicts short-term onset of **severe anemia** (hemoglobin < 8 g/dL) in ICU patients from routinely collected vital signs, laboratory values, fluid/blood inputs, and demographics. The model is **developed** on the ViennaAIdb cohort (Medical University of Vienna) and **externally validated** on the publicly available [MIMIC-IV](https://physionet.org/content/mimiciv/) database.

## Repository structure

The notebooks form an ordered pipeline. Default settings use an hourly sampling grid (`sampling_interval = '1h'`), an 18-hour input sequence (`sequence_len = 18`), a 6-hour prediction offset (`pred_offset = 6`), and a severe-anemia threshold of 8 g/dL (`hb_threshold = 8`).

| Step | Notebook | Purpose |
|------|----------|---------|
| 1 | [file_preprocessing_1.ipynb](file_preprocessing_1.ipynb) | Resample raw ICU Parquet tables to an hourly grid, restricted to ±6 h windows around each patient's hemoglobin measurements. |
| 2 | [file_preprocessing_2.ipynb](file_preprocessing_2.ipynb) | Fuse the hourly resampled tables into a single wide dataset and attach static demographics per encounter. |
| 3 | [file_preprocessing_3.ipynb](file_preprocessing_3.ipynb) | Exploratory checks and visual sanity inspection of the fused dataset. |
| 4 | [data_processing.ipynb](data_processing.ipynb) | Build the ViennaAIdb (development) modelling dataset. |
| 5 | [data_processing-MIMIC.ipynb](data_processing-MIMIC.ipynb) | Build the MIMIC-IV (external validation) modelling dataset. |
| 6 | [modelling_regression-MUW_only_measured.ipynb](modelling_regression-MUW_only_measured.ipynb) | Train and evaluate the model on ViennaAIdb (internal development/test). |
| 7 | [modelling_regression-MIMIC_only_measured.ipynb](modelling_regression-MIMIC_only_measured.ipynb) | External validation of the model on MIMIC-IV. |
| 8 | [plot_distributions.ipynb](plot_distributions.ipynb) · [plot_ROC.ipynb](plot_ROC.ipynb) · [plot_PRC.ipynb](plot_PRC.ipynb) · [plot_calibration.ipynb](plot_calibration.ipynb) | Generate the manuscript figures (cohort distributions, ROC, precision–recall, and calibration curves). |

[mimic_ids.csv](mimic_ids.csv) lists the de-identified MIMIC-IV ICU stay identifiers (surrogate keys) that define the external-validation cohort, provided for reproducibility.

> **Note:** Notebook cell outputs have been cleared, because they contained patient-level records derived from restricted-access data that cannot be redistributed (see *Data availability*). The aggregated, anonymised results are provided instead as the figure files below. The notebooks contain file paths (e.g. `/home/lkapral/hb/data/...`) reflecting the original analysis environment; adjust these to your own data locations before running.

## Data availability

The raw datasets are **not** included in this repository and are restricted-access:

- **ViennaAIdb:** Individual participant data cannot be made publicly available because of institutional ethics, privacy, and data-protection restrictions. Access may be considered upon reasonable request to `lorenz.kapral@meduniwien.ac.at` or `aylin.albrecht@meduniwien.ac.at`, subject to approval by the Medical University of Vienna and completion of a data-use agreement.
- **MIMIC-IV:** Available through [PhysioNet](https://physionet.org/content/mimiciv/) to credentialed users who complete the required training and data-use agreement. Redistribution of MIMIC data (including derived patient-level data) is not permitted under the PhysioNet Credentialed Health Data Use Agreement.

## Ethics

The study was approved by the Institutional Review Board of the Medical University of Vienna (2264/2021). The requirement for informed consent was waived due to the retrospective nature of the study and the use of de-identified data. Use of the MIMIC database complied with MIMIC's data access rules and requirements.
